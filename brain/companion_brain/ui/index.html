The fly's own reinforcement learning. Kenyon cells that fired for the current smell, paired with dopamine from sugar (PAM neurons), lose their synapses onto the MBONs that drive avoidance, so the rewarded smell (grape, odor A) gains approach value; the lemon (odor B) is never rewarded. "Probe odors" replays each smell on a scratch copy of the brain and reports the odor-evoked valence. Weights are shown as a fraction of naive.
