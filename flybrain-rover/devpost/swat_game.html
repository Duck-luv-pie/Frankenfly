Mosquito or Chicken

The swatter sits in the middle. Things fly through it. Shake the badge when a mosquito is under the swatter, and hold still for the chickens. The mosquito can see the swatter, because the thing deciding whether it dodges is 150 real neurons from a fly brain.

Click the screen or press space to swing. On a phone, tap "use motion" and then physically shake it, which is exactly what the badge does with its accelerometer.

How it works

  • mosquitoswat it. +1.
  • chickenlet it walk past. Swat it and you lose a life.
  • the dodgea mosquito bolts when its giant fibre fires. Nothing else can make it leave.
  • shake latea fly is faster than your hand. Shake while it is still approaching and it gets out from under the swatter. Wait until it is dead centre.
  • the barthe mosquito's own eye. Full means it is about to go.

A swatter sitting still is not a threat, so the approach alone never trips anything. The shake is the looming stimulus: the head jumps and the mosquito sees it expand, harder the nearer it is. We measured the circuit at 1800 units of current to fire at all, then 10 to 40 ms to fire, which is faster than the 60 ms your swatter takes to land. So you cannot out-race a fly that sees you coming. You beat it by shaking when it is already under the swatter and has nowhere to go.

150 neurons and 900 synapses of the male Drosophila connectome, fixed-point LIF at 100 Hz, the same arithmetic the badge runs. Each mosquito on screen has its own brain. The chicken has none.
