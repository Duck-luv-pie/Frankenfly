Swatter sprite sheet

Every animal below is drawn from the four shapes the badge actually gives us: rounded boxes, lines, arcs and text. No image files, because four sprites would add 21 KB to a push that already times out. What you see here is what the badge draws, same coordinates, same colours, same screen size. The screen, at badge size

320 by 240. The bar along the bottom is the fly's own eye, the live LC4 and LPLC2 firing rate. When it is nearly full the giant fibre is about to go and you are already too late.

Live screen blob

Every animal is this same blob for the first 180 ms.

The timing measured

The fly escapes at 470 ms on the default ramp. You cannot tell what landed until 180 ms. The gap is your whole decision window, and it is where human go/no-go reaction sits.

The four animals

Colour reads first, size second, the word last. Only the fly has a circuit; the others are cardboard, and the spec says so out loud.

Shape lists

Exact widgets for whoever builds it. Coordinates are offsets from the animal's centre at (160, 116). Every box takes a radius; radius equal to half the height makes a circle or a capsule. One widget pool is reused across types, with unused parts hidden.

AnimalWidgetsParts, offset x, y, w, h, radius, colour

What this costs

  • 14 widgets in the pool, created once in on_enter, far under the badge's 512 cap. The chicken uses the most at 11; the fly uses 9.
  • At most 6 native calls per tick while a round is running: the alarm bar value, the body size and position, and the two eyes. Colours and words change only on a transition, not per frame.
  • No image files, so nothing is added to the push. The app stays at about 15 KB.
  • The legs are line widgets with two points each. They cost nothing to hide and they are most of what makes an insect read as an insect.
